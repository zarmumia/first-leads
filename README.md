# First Leads by Zarmumia

A step-by-step coach that helps freelancers and very young businesses get their first leads without paying for ads.

Instead of spending thousands on a campaign that may not work, you get a direction, a start, real contact with potential clients, and a clear picture of your audience. It takes about 15–20 minutes a day.

By Zarmumia — https://zarmumia.co.il/en/

The method was built in practice, to get past these exact problems, and it brought paying retainers from more than one channel, from people with no prior acquaintance. That is also its principle: spread the risk. Never depend on a single platform.

It works for service providers and for founders. You choose what you are looking for: clients, case-study or pilot partners, collaborations and partnerships, or early users and feedback. The method stays the same; the goal of each conversation, the proof you need and the numbers you track adapt to it.

On LinkedIn and Upwork it looks for client work (projects, retainers, freelance and fractional engagements), not employment.

## How it works

1. **Getting to know you** — a short interview about your business, your ideal client, the words they use, and your voice.
2. **Finding where your clients are** — Facebook groups, LinkedIn, Upwork and communities where they ask for help, checked against each place's rules.
3. **Get ready to be checked** — when a comment works, people open your profile. Make sure they understand what you do in ten seconds, and see proof: at least one piece, ideally three. Nothing to show yet? It helps you build a sample, or set up volunteer work in exchange for a testimonial. Then it builds you a simple one-page landing page and walks you through putting it online for free.
4. **Daily routine** — fresh posts and jobs that fit, scored, with drafts written in your voice. You edit and send.
5. **Follow-ups** — who to get back to today, with a personal message ready.
6. **Weekly review** — what actually brings conversations, and what to change.

## Two rules that never change

- **Platform rules come first.** No automation, no mass messages, no scraping, nothing off Upwork before a contract. Conservative daily limits keep your accounts safe.
- **You send every message.** The system finds, scores and drafts. You read, edit and send. That protects your accounts, and keeps the first conversation in your real voice.

- A folder for your First Leads files: your profile, your channels, and your lead log (`leads-log.csv`, opens in Excel or Google Sheets).

Say: **"Start First Leads"**.

## Examples

1. **Start:** "Start First Leads". The coach explains the program, then interviews you about your business and builds your profile.
2. **Daily routine:** "Today's leads". You get follow-ups due today, then up to five fresh posts or jobs that fit, each with a score, why it fits, and a draft in your voice. You edit and send; it logs what you sent.
3. **Weekly review:** "Weekly review". You get a short table of which places bring conversations, what your best replies have in common, and up to three changes for next week.

## What it reads, stores and sends

- **Reads:** only pages you are logged in to and choose (your Facebook groups, LinkedIn searches, Upwork searches, forums), through the Claude in Chrome extension, only while you are there and ask for a session, one page at a time, at a human pace, about 10 pages per channel per session. It never scans in the background or on a schedule, and you can always open a page yourself and say "check this page". It does not open members' profiles or collect contact details.
- **Stores:** plain files in a folder you choose on your computer: `profile.md`, `channels.md`, `leads-log.csv`, `progress.md`, and your landing page. Nothing else.
- **Sends:** nothing. It never posts, comments, messages, connects, applies or submits. It has no servers, no tracking and no connections to other services. You send every message yourself.

## Privacy

Your data stays in your files and your Claude account. Zarmumia receives nothing. The lead log stores no names, emails or phone numbers: only a link to the post or job, where it was, and a short summary of what was asked. Delete the folder to delete everything.
